# Before you publish a clip

- [ ] The opening makes sense without the full episode.
- [ ] The clip delivers the answer or payoff it introduces.
- [ ] The edit preserves the speaker's qualifications and meaning.
- [ ] Names, numbers, negatives, and technical terms in captions are correct.
- [ ] Faces and important visual information remain in frame.
- [ ] Captions remain readable on a phone and clear of interface overlays.
- [ ] Audio is intelligible and does not clip.
- [ ] You have the rights or permission needed to publish the footage.
- [ ] The downloaded export plays from beginning to end.
- [ ] You retained a local copy before the hosted file expires.
