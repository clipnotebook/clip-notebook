Privacy & contact

This resource library is a static website. It has no account system, video upload form, or payment processing.

What this page uses

We do not install analytics or advertising scripts on this library. GitHub Pages serves the site and may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy policy. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives requests from your browser. The checklist operates in page memory and is not sent to us.

Links to other services

Links to our tools use campaign parameters to identify referrals. Following those links takes you to a different service with its own privacy policy. This library is operated by the team behind OpenShorts.org and Snazo.org.

Contact

For corrections or questions, open a GitHub issue. Do not include private videos, credentials, or payment information.
